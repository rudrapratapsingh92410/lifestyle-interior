## ✨ Features & The 6 Transformation Pillars

1. **Luxury Editorial Homepage**
   - High-contrast obsidian & champagne gold aesthetic (`#0c0d0e` & `#c5a059`).
   - Cormorant Garamond serif typography paired with Plus Jakarta Sans.
   - Headline: *“Spaces That Feel Like They Were Made for You.”*

2. **Interactive Before & After Transformation Slider**
   - Draggable split-screen comparison engine (touch & mouse compatible).
   - Allows prospective clients to inspect raw concrete structural constraints vs. completed luxury interiors with Italian marble and concealed lighting.

3. **Interactive Project Portfolio**
   - Category filtering: *Luxury Apartments, Heritage & Modern Villas, Gourmet Modular Kitchens, and Commercial Chambers*.

4. **Story-Driven Project Case Studies**

5. **Interactive Design DNA Discovery Tool**
