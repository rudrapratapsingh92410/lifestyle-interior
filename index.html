The Designer's Blueprint

We Sell a Disciplined Process, Not Just Photographs.

Most interior designers show you pretty pictures without revealing the messy structural reality. At Lifestyle Interior, every square foot is engineered through a 4-step transparent delivery framework.

Phase 01 • Spatial Diagnostic & Brief Day 1 - 7

Laser 3D scanning of your Kolkata residence. We assess electrical load limits, natural daylight angles, and your lifestyle routines.

Phase 02 • 3D Hyper-Render & Material Box Day 8 - 20

Touch physical Italian marble samples, veneers, and fabric swatches at our Kidderpore studio before committing to production.

Phase 03 • Factory Joinery & On-Site Execution Day 21 - 50

Precision CNC cutting in off-site facilities ensures minimal dust and zero neighborhood disruption in your building.

Phase 04 • The White-Glove Handover Day 55

Deep cleaning, styling, 10-year warranty certificate, and digital maintenance vault delivered in person.
